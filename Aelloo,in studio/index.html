FAQ

The questions
everyone asks.

How long does a typical project take?

Starter sites ship in 2 weeks. Studio projects in 4. Enterprise engagements run 6–10 weeks depending on scope. I share a detailed timeline during the proposal stage — no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside India?

No this studio is new. I'll timezone-flexible and run async-first with overlap hours for live calls.

What's your tech stack?

Hand-crafted HTML, CSS and vanilla JS by default — fastest possible sites, zero bloat. also I use AI to make your work faster

Do you only build, or do you also help with strategy?

Both, and I strongly recommend strategy-first engagements. The websites that convert best are the ones where positioning, messaging and design were built together — not stitched after.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of post-launch care. After that, most clients move to a monthly retainer for ongoing optimization, A/B testing and small enhancements.

Can I see more work before I commit?

Of course — I'll send a private case-study deck tailored to your industry on our intro call. Most prospects find one specific project that maps closely to what they need.
